How it helps

Start with what’s on your mind.

MHM turns a conversation into practical support. It remembers the details, keeps today manageable, and delivers reminders with enough variation to stay useful.

01

Talk to MHM

Ask for a reminder, add a task, save a note, start a check-in, or simply explain what feels hard. You do not need to choose the right tool first.

02

Let MHM remember

Keep reminders, tasks, notes, check-ins, and useful context together. For scheduled support, choose a window like 9–11 a.m.; MHM varies the message and timing within it so the nudge stays noticeable while still fitting your day.

03

Keep moving on hard days

Missed something? MHM helps you defer, skip, simplify, or try again without turning one hard day into a failed system.

The idea behind MHM

Productivity tools usually assume the problem is discipline.

Sometimes the problem is capacity.

People forget things. Energy changes. Attention disappears. Routines break. Hard days happen.

MHM is being built around a different assumption: a useful system should help you adapt when life stops matching the plan.

That means support without shame, structure without rigidity, and reminders that understand “not today” can be a valid answer.

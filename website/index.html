How it helps

Support that fits your day and stays fresh.

A reminder at exactly the same time every day can become easy to tune out. MHM adds a little variation within your chosen windows, while keeping reminders, tasks, and check-ins together.

01

Remember for me

Keep track of routines, health reminders, tasks, and the small things that are easy to lose when your brain is already full.

02

Keep it fresh

Choose a window, like 9–11 a.m. MHM varies the messages and sends them at slightly randomized times within it. Enough variety to help them stay noticeable, with timing that still fits your life.

03

Help me restart

Missed something? MHM helps you defer, skip, simplify, or try again without turning one hard day into a failed system.

What lives inside MHM

A practical toolkit for keeping life moving.

Each part is useful on its own. Together, they give MHM enough context to be genuinely helpful.

✦

Motivational messages

Varied encouragement delivered at slightly randomized times within your chosen windows. A fresh nudge, without needing to be available at one exact time every day.

♡

Health reminders

Gentle reminders for medication, hydration, sleep routines, appointments, movement, or whatever matters to you.

◌

Check-ins

Track things like mood, hope, energy, stress, meals, outdoors time, water, and bedtime — without turning your life into a spreadsheet.

✓

Tasks that bend

Do it now, defer it, skip it, simplify it. MHM is built around continuing forward, not maintaining a perfect streak.

▤

Notebook

Keep notes, lists, logs, and journal entries together with shared tags, dates, and context so you can actually find them again.

The idea behind MHM

Productivity tools usually assume the problem is discipline.

Sometimes the problem is capacity.

People forget things. Energy changes. Attention disappears. Routines break. Hard days happen.

MHM is being built around a different assumption: a useful system should help you adapt when life stops matching the plan.

That means support without shame, structure without rigidity, and reminders that understand “not today” can be a valid answer.
